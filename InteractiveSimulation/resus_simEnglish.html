          About & Educational Safety

          ResusSim is an educational simulation tool. It is designed to help healthcare learners practice resuscitation decision-making aligned with the 2025 AHA Guidelines for CPR & ECC.

          It is not a substitute for:

          • Formal American Heart Association course completion (BLS, ACLS, PALS, etc.) and hands-on skills verification
          • Your institution's specific resuscitation protocols and equipment
          • Direct medical supervision and physician judgment
          • Real-time clinical judgment on an actual patient

          All medications, doses, timings, and algorithm steps in this tool are presented for practice purposes and reflect a good-faith educational summary of the 2025 AHA update. Always verify against the official published 2025 AHA algorithms and your local protocols before applying anything learned here in clinical practice.
